What We Do

Services

Discover a comprehensive suite of services at AGL Painting LLC, your trusted partner for all your painting and construction needs, including interior and exterior painting, drywall, tape and float, texture, expert color matching, faux finish, custom texture, metal stud framing, acoustical ceilings, demolition, sandblasting, and pressure washing, ensuring exceptional results for every project.

Roof Coating System

A cost-effective alternative to roof replacement with our Seamless Roof Coating System. This fluid-applied solution forms a durable, waterproof barrier that helps extend the life of your existing roof while saving you money. With experienced professionals, superior workmanship, and full licensing, AGL Painting delivers reliable protection and long-lasting results.

Epoxy Floor Coating

Our epoxy floor coatings offer long lasting protection, a beautiful appearance, and easy maintenance. Whether for residential or commercial use, our epoxy coatings deliver both style and strength.

Interior & Exterior Painting

Transform your space with our comprehensive Interior & Exterior painting services, bringing vibrant colors and flawless finishes to every surface.

Demolition, Metal Stud Framing & Drywall

Experience unparalleled craftsmanship with our Drywall, Tape & Float, Texture services. Our skilled professionals excel in creating seamless surfaces, from precision taping and floating to adding custom textures that breathe life into your spaces. Whether it's a Level 5 finish or intricate texturing, we deliver results that exceed expectations, ensuring a flawless foundation for the perfect paint job.

Sandblasting & Pressure Washing

Industrial surface preparation using sandblasting and pressure washing to ensure maximum coating adhesion, cleanliness, and longevity on any substrate — with meticulous attention to detail.

Faux Finish

Elevate your space with the artistry of Faux Finish, where our skilled touch brings captivating textures and visual depth to your walls.

Custom Texture

Experience the touch of innovation through our Custom Texture service, where we transform ordinary surfaces into captivating expressions of style and uniqueness.

Acoustical Ceilings

Elevate both aesthetics and acoustics with our Acoustical Ceilings service, where form and function harmonize to create a truly immersive environment.
